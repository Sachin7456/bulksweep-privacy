BulkSweep for Instagram — Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

BulkSweep is a Chrome extension made by toolsgoat. It removes your own Instagram likes and comments on Instagram's Your activity pages.

What it reads

While you run it on instagram.com, BulkSweep reads the Your activity page in your browser tab: Instagram's buttons, the items listed, and Instagram's answers to the Unlike and Delete actions it presses for you. It does this only to do what you asked, and only in that tab.

What it stores

Nothing about you. While a run continues across a page reload it keeps, in that tab only, the job you chose and its progress (counts and times). This is removed as soon as the next page reads it and ends with the tab. No media IDs, names, messages or content are stored.

What it sends

Nothing. BulkSweep has no server and makes no network requests of its own. Only Instagram's own page talks to Instagram, exactly as when you press its buttons yourself. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Permissions

None at install. If you turn on notifications, Chrome shows one notification with the result line when a run ends. You can turn them off on the extension's welcome page at any time.

Sharing and sale

We do not collect your data, so we do not share or sell it, and we do not use it for any purpose other than the one above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
