BulkSweep for Instagram

Unlike all your Instagram likes and delete your comments in bulk. Posts or reels, newest or oldest first, all or just a number. A Chrome extension: no password, nothing stored or sent.

  • Batches with breaks, waits for Instagram's answer before the next one
  • Stops at once if Instagram says "Try again later"
  • No permission at install; notifications only if you ask
